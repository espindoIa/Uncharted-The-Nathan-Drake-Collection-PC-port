**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port by **BilalReloaded**
([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BilalReloaded/videos), [GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983)).

On 0.2.0 or older? The launcher offers this update: press **UPDATE TO 0.2.1** next to PLAY.

## Fixed

- **NVIDIA DLAA and AMD FSR 4 Native AA were missing from the 0.2.0 download.** The package build
  could not fetch their files, so the in-game menu (Image -> Anti-aliasing) and the launcher only
  offered TAA and FSR 3.1. They are in this package: **DLAA** on NVIDIA RTX cards, **FSR 4** on GPUs
  that can run its 8-bit model.

Everything else is as in 0.2.0: anti-aliasing, ultrawide, smooth frame pacing above 60 FPS with
VSync, the Uncharted 3 rooftop freeze and crash fixes, controls in the in-game menu with your own
menu shortcut, and the rest: see the
[0.2.0 release](https://github.com/bc160201983/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
and [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/bc160201983/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install (new players)

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (the dumped `CUSA02344` folder with
   `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Recommended: update 1.02 next to it, named like it plus `-patch`.
3. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own dump of the Collection (CUSA02344). Not
> affiliated with Sony, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint Games.

GNU GPL v2 or later; the zip's `licenses` folder lists every bundled library. DLAA uses NVIDIA DLSS
(`nvngx_dlss.dll`, under NVIDIA's license in `licenses/nvidia-dlss`); NVIDIA, RTX and DLSS are
trademarks of NVIDIA Corporation.
