**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port, **Expedition Edition**: the fork by
[espindoIa](https://github.com/espindoIa/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port) of
BilalReloaded's port ([GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port)),
with its 0.4.0 merged in.

## New in 0.5.0

- **No more freezes of 1-6 seconds after some cutscenes** in Uncharted 3 and 2. Your shader cache
  does not need to be cleared.
- **Chapter and area loads stutter less** in Uncharted 3, and the stall after a pre-rendered movie
  should be shorter.
- **No hitch on the first frame of gameplay with DLSS** in Uncharted 3: DLSS is now prepared while
  you are in the menus.
- **Faster in busy places:** the Cartagena market in chapter 2 runs about 3 FPS faster, chapter 2
  no longer gets stuck at a lower frame rate after the alley checkpoint, and Uncharted 3 is a bit
  faster with multithreaded rendering.
- **New Ultra Quality upscaling mode**, between DLAA and Quality. Cutscenes run at full speed
  again at 4K.
- **Each game keeps its own shader cache**, copied once from the old shared one, so nothing is
  recompiled.
- **Safer saves:** written atomically, and backed up at every launch (the newest 5 are kept).
- **Launcher and settings fixes:** settings survive a crash or a kill, your upscaler choice is not
  overwritten when it cannot run, a failed update keeps the installed version, "Apply and restart"
  works, and menu keys no longer stick.
- **Stability fixes from a code audit:** memory races, a movie of another size corrupting memory, a
  timer hang, and a broken 15.8 GB texture ending in "Device lost".

## From BilalReloaded's 0.4.0

No more freeze before some cutscenes; Drake's Fortune's enemy grenade launchers no longer blow up on
Drake; Infinite ammo keeps the magazine full. Its Upscaling option and its switch that turns
multithreaded rendering off for Uncharted 3 are not taken.

## Known issues

- Heavy Uncharted 3 areas (chapter 2, after the movie) are limited by the CPU: 52-80 FPS in one log.
- A rare long stall inside the graphics driver (188 and 266 ms seen) is still open. The log now has
  a "Slow submit detail:" line; please send the log if you see one.
- Most of the Uncharted 3 stutter fixes were tested only in Uncharted 3.

Everything that changed: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/espindoIa/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (the dumped `CUSA02320` or
   `CUSA02344` folder with `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Recommended: update 1.02 next to it, named
   like it plus `-patch`, or chosen in Advanced -> GAME UPDATE.
3. For DLSS/DLAA, the launcher asks for NVIDIA's `nvngx_dlss.dll` (not included: NVIDIA's license).
4. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own dump of the Collection. Not affiliated with
> Sony, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint Games.
