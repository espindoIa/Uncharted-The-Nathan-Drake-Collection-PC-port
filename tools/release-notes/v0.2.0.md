**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port by **BilalReloaded**
([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BilalReloaded/videos), [GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983)).

A big one: anti-aliasing with NVIDIA DLAA and AMD FSR, ultrawide, much smoother frame pacing,
crash and freeze fixes in all three games, and full control rebinding in the in-game menu.

On 0.1.x? The launcher offers this update: press **UPDATE TO 0.2.0** next to PLAY.

## Fixed

- **Crashes in all three games (#11, #12, #17):** the most reported crash (`m_gfxEopTick` in
  FrameBegin, after short stalls) no longer stops the game; it waits for the GPU frame instead.
- **Uncharted 3:**
  - freeze on the **Cartagena rooftops** (chapter 2), about 30 seconds into the chase;
  - crash climbing the **Citadel** in chapter 8 (#13);
  - **Sully's shirt** exploding into flat-coloured triangles on NVIDIA GPUs (#9), and the GPU
    crash ("Device lost") when young Sully appears after the museum (#18);
  - Nate's legs flickering and NPCs floating a little;
  - stopping shortly after a level starts on **AMD integrated GPUs** (not seen any more in a
    6-minute test; tell us if it still happens).
- **Cutscenes above 60 FPS** played too fast and out of sync with their sound.
- **Hitches after loading:** the shader cache now loads in the background at the title screen, so
  shaders are no longer compiled during play (Uncharted 3 had 90-170 ms hitches after each load).

## Smoother and faster

- **Even frame pacing above 60 FPS with VSync:** with a frame rate limit at or above your monitor's
  refresh rate, the game now follows the monitor. On a 75 Hz monitor at the 120 setting: an even
  75 FPS, **1% lows 32 -> 66 FPS** (Uncharted 3) and **22 -> 67 FPS** (Uncharted 2), no frame
  over 20 ms (28-35% before). New frame rate limits **75, 90 and 100** for such monitors and
  variable refresh screens.
- **Much faster at 4K and in crowds** (Ryzen 9 9950X3D + RTX 5080): Uncharted 2's heaviest view
  38 -> 99 FPS, Uncharted 3's Cartagena crowd 69 -> 90, Drake's Fortune 95 -> 101.
- **Multithreaded rendering is much faster:** 4K Uncharted 3 100 FPS, Uncharted 2 118.
- **Low-end PCs:** the games no longer take every core of a 4-6 core CPU (idle game threads
  sleep): 4 cores 60 FPS at the title (was 19-27), integrated GPUs 27 FPS at 720p (was 5-7).
  New **540p** render resolution for integrated GPUs.

## Lowest PC that plays

- **Needs:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); a CPU from **Intel's 4th-generation Core (Haswell, 2013)
  or newer, or any AMD Ryzen** (the games need AVX, F16C, BMI1, MOVBE and LZCNT); a graphics card
  or integrated GPU with **Vulkan 1.3** and a current driver; about **6 GB of free RAM** for the
  game (16 GB in the PC recommended).
- **Weakest GPU tested: the integrated AMD Radeon graphics of a Ryzen 7000/9000 desktop CPU**
  (2 compute units, about a quarter of a PS4's GPU). At **720p**: Uncharted 3 27-39 FPS,
  Uncharted 2 27 FPS, Drake's Fortune 24 FPS; at **540p** Uncharted 3 33 FPS. Playable, not
  smooth. Settings: preset **Low**, render resolution **540p or 720p** with FSR 1, anti-aliasing
  off.
- **4 CPU cores:** 60 FPS in Drake's Fortune and Uncharted 3 (title screen and in play; a 4-core
  test with a fast GPU), with **Idle game threads: Sleep** (the default). On a 4-6 core CPU also
  try **Multithreaded rendering** (per game) and keep **Frames queued at 1**.
- **Players report:** Core i5-10400F + RTX 3050 6 GB plays Uncharted 2 and 3.

## New

- **Anti-aliasing** (in-game menu -> Image, or launcher -> Display): **TAA**, **AMD FSR 3.1 Native
  AA**, **AMD FSR 4 Native AA** and **NVIDIA DLAA** (RTX), in all three games, at any render
  resolution. Edge shimmer is roughly halved (DLAA: a third). DLAA uses NVIDIA DLSS technology.
- **Ultrawide (#7):** launcher -> Display -> **Aspect ratio** 21:9 or 32:9. More to the sides,
  nothing stretched; the HUD and menus keep their layout.
- **Controls in the in-game menu:** rebind every action for keyboard, mouse and controller, mouse
  sensitivity and invert, button icons, camera speed. **The menu's own shortcut is yours too
  (#15, #19):** LT + R3, View + Menu, none, or any buttons you pick; and any keyboard key instead
  of Insert.
- **Keyboard/mouse and Xbox button icons** in the games' hints and menus.
- **Raw mouse look** in Uncharted 2 and 3.
- **Aim sensitivity sliders** go 4-5 times higher (game options -> Controls -> Aiming).
- **Draw distance** sliders (Uncharted 2 and 3, experimental): object fade, sun and lamp shadows.

## Known issues

- **Drake's Fortune:** the grenade launcher can kill the player (#6). Still being looked at.
- **Frames queued 2** is faster in heavy scenes, but GPU results reach the game a frame later
  (Uncharted 2's NPCs float): keep it at 1.
- **Recording with OBS Game Capture:** if OBS keeps re-hooking the game, its memory use grows. With
  an NVIDIA card set NVIDIA Control Panel -> Manage 3D settings -> Program settings ->
  Uncharted.exe -> **Vulkan/OpenGL present method: Prefer native**, or use OBS **Window Capture**.
- Report problems with the log from the launcher's **Advanced -> Open log folder**.

## Install (new players)

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (the dumped `CUSA02344` folder with
   `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Recommended: update 1.02 next to it, named like it plus `-patch`.
3. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own dump of the Collection (CUSA02344). Not
