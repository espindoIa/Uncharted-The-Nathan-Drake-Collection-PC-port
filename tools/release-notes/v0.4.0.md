**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port, **Expedition Edition**: the fork by
[espindoIa](https://github.com/espindoIa/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port) of
BilalReloaded's port ([GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port)),
with its 0.3.1 merged in.

## New in this fork

- **Its own launcher:** night-blue look with a signal-flare orange, a compass icon, and updates from
  this fork's releases.
- **US version (CUSA02320) with update 1.02 tested:** all three games start and play, with every
  fix of the fork applied. Europe's CUSA02344 works as before.
- **DLSS / DLAA in all three games**, with the DLSS model to choose in the launcher and the in-game
  menu.
- **Multithreaded rendering in all three games**, with the Uncharted 3 freezes and crashes it had
  fixed.
- **Smoother frame pacing:** frames go to the screen as soon as they are drawn, and Uncharted 2 no
  longer stutters regularly at the GPU's limit.
- **Cutscene movies** paced by game time above 60 FPS and sharper at 4K.

## From BilalReloaded's 0.3.1

Sun shadows no longer flicker with TAA, FSR or DLSS/DLAA; steadier anti-aliasing; the game update
chosen in the launcher (folder or .pkg); 16:10 for Steam Deck; movies keep their shape at 16:10 and
ultrawide; fixes for AMD RX 9070 XT pipelines and Linux AMD/Intel GPUs.

Everything that changed: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/espindoIa/Uncharted-The-Nathan-Drake-Collection-PC-port/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (the dumped `CUSA02320` or
   `CUSA02344` folder with `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Recommended: update 1.02 next to it, named
   like it plus `-patch`, or chosen in Advanced -> GAME UPDATE.
3. For DLSS/DLAA, the launcher asks for NVIDIA's `nvngx_dlss.dll` (not included: NVIDIA's license).
4. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own dump of the Collection. Not affiliated with
> Sony, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint Games.
